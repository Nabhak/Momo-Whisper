What Momo adds

More than dictation.
A whole writing sidekick.

Speak to Translate

Hold Fn+Shift and talk — your words come out translated into any of 16 languages, with the source auto-detected.

Highlight to Fix

Select text anywhere and a floating popup offers Translate, Grammar, or Rewrite — the result lands right in place.

Tone Control

Six tones for Thai & English — Email, Professional, Workplace Chat, Casual, AI-prompt, No-filter — so the wording fits the moment.

Learns Your Words

A custom dictionary fixes names and jargon the recognizer keeps missing — teach it once, it catches the mis-hears forever.

Fast with Groq

whisper-large-v3 for speech, gpt-oss-120b for translation — one Groq key powers it all, generous free tier included.

Light & Private

Pure Swift, no Electron. Your key stays on your Mac and your audio/text only ever go to the Groq call you configured.

How to install

  1. Download Momo Whisper, unzip, and drag Momo Whisper into your Applications folder.
  2. First launch. macOS blocks it once with “Apple could not verify…” — expected on any app that isn't paid-notarized. Do this one time and it opens normally forever after:
    1. Double-click the app → on the warning, click Done.
    2. Open Privacy & Security (or open System Settings → Privacy & Security yourself).
    3. Scroll down to “Momo Whisper was blocked…” and click Open Anyway.
    4. Enter your password, then click Open.

    — or, in Terminal —

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Momo Whisper.app"
  3. Set it up. On first run the app asks for two macOS permissions and your API key:
    1. Allow Microphone — so it can hear you dictate.
    2. Allow Accessibility — so it can type the result into other apps.
    3. Get a free key at console.groq.com and paste it in Settings (or set GROQ_API_KEY in ~/.zshrc).
  4. Use it. Hold Fn to dictate · Fn+Shift to translate · or highlight any text and pick a mode.
