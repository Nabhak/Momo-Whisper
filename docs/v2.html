In case you're wondering

The usual questions.

Is it actually free?
Yep. The app is free and open source. You just bring your own Groq key — and Groq has a generous free tier — so there's nothing to pay us.
What's this Groq key thing?
It's a free key from console.groq.com that powers the speech and translation. Grab one, paste it in on first launch, and Momo walks you through the rest.
Which languages can it do?
Thai and English are the most polished (with tone control), plus 14 more — Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German and others — for both talking and translating.
Where does my voice go?
Only to the Groq call you set up with your own key. There's no Momo server in the middle, and we keep nothing.
My Mac says it can't verify the app?
That's just the standard warning for apps that aren't paid-notarized. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and hit Open Anyway once — smooth sailing after that.
