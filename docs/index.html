How it works

1

Hold Fn and speak — released, it's transcribed by Whisper and typed into the app you're in

2

Hold Fn+⇧ instead — your speech is translated into your chosen language and typed out

3

Or highlight text in any app and click the little translate button — the result pops up next to it

What Momo adds

🌐

Speak to Translate

Hold Fn+Shift and talk — your words come out translated into any of 16 languages (Thai, English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French & more), with the source auto-detected.

✍️

Highlight to Translate

Select text in any app, click the floating button, and the translation appears in place — pick any of the 16 target languages right in the popup.

🎭

Tone Control

Six tones for Thai & English — Email, Professional, Workplace Chat, Casual, AI-prompt, and No-filter — so the wording fits the moment. Other languages translate in a clean, natural style.

📖

Learns Your Words

A custom dictionary fixes names and jargon the recognizer keeps missing — teach a word once and it catches the mis-spellings.

⚡️

Fast with Groq

whisper-large-v3 for speech, gpt-oss-120b for translation — one Groq key powers it all, with a generous free tier and quota-aware fallback.

🎙️

Global Hotkey

Fn hold-to-talk by default, fully rebindable to any modifier or combo. Lives in the menu bar — no Dock clutter.

📋

Types Anywhere

Clipboard-safe auto-paste into whatever app you're in, with a live waveform and status overlay while it works.

🪶

Light & Private

Pure Swift, no Electron. Your key is stored locally and your audio/text only ever go to the Groq call you configured.

How to install

  1. Download Momo Whisper, unzip, and drag Momo Whisper into your Applications folder.
  2. First launch. macOS blocks it once with “Apple could not verify…” — expected on any app that isn't paid-notarized. Do this one time and it opens normally forever after:
    1. Double-click the app → on the warning, click Done.
    2. Open Privacy & Security (or open System Settings → Privacy & Security yourself).
    3. Scroll down to “Momo Whisper was blocked…” and click Open Anyway.
    4. Enter your password, then click Open.

    — or, in Terminal —

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Momo Whisper.app"
  3. Set it up. On first run the app asks for two macOS permissions and your API key:
    1. Allow Microphone — so it can hear you dictate.
    2. Allow Accessibility — so it can type the result into other apps.
    3. Get a free key at console.groq.com and paste it in Settings (or set GROQ_API_KEY in ~/.zshrc).
  4. Use it. Hold Fn to dictate · Fn+Shift to translate · or highlight any text and click the translate button.
