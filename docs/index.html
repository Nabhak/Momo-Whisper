Fast with Groq

whisper-large-v3 for speech, gpt-oss-120b for translation. One Groq key powers it all, with a generous free tier and quota-aware fallback.

Global Hotkey

Fn hold-to-talk by default, fully rebindable to any modifier or combo. Lives in the menu bar, no Dock clutter.

Types Anywhere

Clipboard-safe auto-paste into whatever app you're in, with a live waveform and status overlay while it works.

Light & Private

Pure Swift, no Electron. Your key is stored locally and your audio and text only ever go to the Groq call you configured.

Tone Control

The right words for the moment.

Six tones for Thai and English: Email, Professional, Workplace Chat, Casual, AI-prompt, and No-filter.

Other languages translate in a clean, natural style. Pick a tone once and every dictation comes out sounding like it should.

Teach a word once and it catches the mis-spellings from then on. Product names, teammates, Thai nicknames, all of it.

Expected on any app that isn't paid-notarized. Do this once and it opens normally forever after:

  1. Double-click the app, then click Done on the warning.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to "Momo Whisper was blocked…" and click Open Anyway.
